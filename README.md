# 📊 AI Business Analyst Dashboard

> Transform raw business Excel reports into executive dashboards, business insights, and downloadable reports in seconds.

---

## 🚀 Overview

AI Business Analyst Dashboard is a browser-based Business Intelligence application that automates the repetitive reporting work typically performed by Business Analysts for Category Managers.

Instead of manually filtering Excel sheets, creating pivot tables, calculating KPIs, generating charts, and preparing reports every morning, users simply upload an Excel file and receive a complete executive dashboard with downloadable reports.

The application is built entirely using **HTML**, **CSS**, and **Vanilla JavaScript** and runs completely in the browser.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Every morning, Business Analysts typically perform the following tasks:

Database / Excel

↓

Filter Yesterday's Data

↓

Calculate KPIs

↓

Create Pivot Tables

↓

Generate Charts

↓

Analyze Performance

↓

Prepare Executive Reports

↓

Share Reports with Category Managers

This process is repetitive, time-consuming, and prone to manual errors.

The AI Business Analyst Dashboard automates this workflow.

# ⭐ Project Goal

The goal of this project is to automate repetitive business reporting and provide Category Managers with actionable insights through a fast, browser-based Business Intelligence dashboard.

Instead of spending hours preparing reports, users can upload an Excel file and receive executive dashboards, KPIs, visualizations, business insights, and downloadable reports within seconds.
